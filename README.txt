## Important
The supplied real prices are retained:
- Straight/basic haircut: ₹180
- Advanced haircut: ₹350
- Split ends: ₹250
- Bangs: ₹80

Hair wash ₹100 and other prices are portfolio proposal prices and must be confirmed by the client before public launch.
